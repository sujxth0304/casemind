# CASEMIND

AI-Assisted Investigative Intelligence Platform.

CASEMIND is a research and engineering project exploring how AI,
knowledge graphs, information retrieval, computer vision, and
multimodal evidence processing can assist investigators in analyzing
large collections of heterogeneous evidence.

## Project Goals

- Evidence ingestion and provenance
- Entity extraction and resolution
- Cross-case correlation
- Knowledge graph construction
- Timeline reconstruction
- Hybrid semantic and keyword search
- Evidence-grounded RAG
- Multimedia evidence analysis
- Human-in-the-loop investigation workflows
- Auditable AI outputs

## Responsible AI

CASEMIND is an experimental decision-support system.

It does not determine guilt, innocence, criminal intent, or
appropriate legal action.

All investigative conclusions must remain subject to human review.
